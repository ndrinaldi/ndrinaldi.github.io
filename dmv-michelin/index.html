    Don't chase these

    Starred in the 2025 guide, gone since:

    • Gravitas — seized by DC's tax office in January 2026 over unpaid taxes; the equipment went to auction in April.
    • Tail Up Goat — closed 20 December 2025 after ten years. The same owners reopened the Adams Morgan space as Rye Bunny, counter-service, in April 2026.
    • Kinship and Métier — Eric Ziebold closed both in December 2025 rather than re-sign the lease.

    Tap a pin for the details. Some of these are on top of each other — Jônt is directly above Bresca — so pins fan apart to stay tappable and a clustered one sits a few metres off its real address. Everything is in DC bar the Inn at Little Washington, 67 miles west in the Blue Ridge, about 90 minutes each way.
