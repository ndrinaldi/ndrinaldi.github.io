NOVA Pizza, Compared

Arlington to Leesburg, twenty places worth the drive, arranged the way you actually choose: what kind of pie you want, how far you're willing to go, and whether this is a date or a Tuesday.

Updated 16 Aug 2026 4 min read

The short version

Stop asking which NOVA pizza is best. Six different styles are each done well here, mostly by different people, and picking the style first makes the choice obvious in about five seconds.

Pick a style

Decide what kind of pizza you want first

Everything downstream follows from this. A Neapolitan place and a slice shop are not competing for the same night — they barely sell the same food. Six styles have a real champion in Northern Virginia.

Neapolitan · the benchmark

A Modo Mio

Yorktown, Arlington. Vera Pizza Napoletana certified, oven built in Naples, and the only Virginia or DC pizzeria on 50 Top Pizza's 2026 list of the best in the US — at No. 37, after No. 32 the year before and No. 48 in 2023. Soupy-centred Margherita, charred puffy cornicione. This is the highest ceiling in the region.

New Haven coal · the outlier

Frank Pepe

Duke Street, Alexandria. The 1925 New Haven original opened a branch here in 2022 — a coal oven at 650–800°F making apizza you genuinely cannot get anywhere else nearby. Order the white clam or you have wasted the trip.

NY slice · the workhorse

Andy's Pizza

Ballston, Tysons, McLean, Fairfax, Old Town. Three-day cold ferment, crisp-and-elastic, sold by the slice around $4.50. Won the Worldwide Pizza Championships in 2021 and a gold at the Pizza Expo for plain cheese. Some find the sauce sweet — that's the one caveat.

Detroit square · the group order

Nighthawk Brewery & Pizza

Westpost, Arlington. Thick Detroit squares with cheese to the edge, hand-tossed thin as the alternative, 10,000 square feet of room, and Aslin brewing on site. $5 NY slices at weekday happy hour.

Roman · the chef-driven one

Stracci Pizza

Del Ray, Alexandria. Airy, crisp sourdough rectangles, stracciatella pulled in house, toppings that run to roasted peaches, black truffle and bone marrow. The most "restaurant" pizza on this page.

Bar pie · the thin one

Colony Grill

Clarendon, Arlington. Connecticut-style 12-inch bar pies, cracker-thin, and the hot-oil topping that the whole cult is built around. Order two per person; they are small on purpose.

Buffalo cup-and-char is arguably a seventh — Badd Pizza in Falls Church and McLean builds it on recipes sourced from Buffalo's Stevie Badd, and nobody else nearby is really trying.

Where you are

Nearest good option, by corridor

Most of the density is on the Orange/Silver line through Arlington. If you're already out west, the list gets short fast — which is itself the useful information.

  • Rosslyn – Ballston Wiseguy and Ravenna in Rosslyn, Stellina at N. Moore, then Ballston Local, Timber, We The Pizza and Andy's clustered around Ballston. Highest choice-per-square-mile in the region.
  • Clarendon – Courthouse Colony Grill for bar pies, Fire Works for wood-fired, Big Tony's if you want a dive bar attached to the pizza.
  • North Arlington – Langston Blvd A Modo Mio and Pie-tanza in Yorktown, Old Dominion Pizza Co. on Cherry Hill, The Italian Store in Lyon Village. The quiet best of the bunch.
  • National Landing – Pentagon City Crush, District Pizza Palace, We The Pizza and Wiseguy; Nighthawk a few blocks south at Westpost.
  • Alexandria Frank Pepe on Duke Street, Lena's by Braddock Road Metro, Stracci in Del Ray. Three completely different pies within about ten minutes.
  • Falls Church – McLean – Tysons Badd Pizza on both ends, Pizzeria Orso in Falls Church, Stellina at Capital One Center, Andy's in Tysons and McLean.
  • Fairfax and west Pupatella in the Mosaic District is the standout; past that, Fire Works in Leesburg, which has taken "Best Pizza in Loudoun" in the Loudoun Times-Mirror poll seven years running.

By situation

If you only have one question, it's probably this one

One meal in NOVA, best possible pizza — where?

A Modo Mio, Langston Blvd in Arlington. It's the only pizzeria in Virginia or DC on 50 Top Pizza's 2026 US list, and it has been on that list three cycles running. Go at weekday lunch, when a pie runs under $13.

Something I can't get anywhere else?

Frank Pepe's white clam pie on Duke Street in Alexandria. Coal-fired New Haven apizza, fresh clams, no mozzarella. It divides people, which is the point.

Feeding eight people, including kids, with beer?

Nighthawk at Westpost — Detroit squares, thin-crust for the holdouts, Aslin beer brewed on the premises, and enough room that nobody minds a loud table. Stracci's $37-per-person family menu is the grown-up version of the same idea, for parties of eight or more.

A slice, on foot, on the way home?

Andy's if you're near Ballston, Tysons, McLean, Fairfax or Old Town. Wiseguy in Rosslyn or National Landing if you want it foldable and a bit weirder — the paneer tikka slice is not a gimmick.

Date night, but still pizza?

Stracci in Del Ray for the chef-driven Roman pies, or Lena's near Braddock Road if you want a wood oven, a proper drinks list and a heated patio. Stellina in Shirlington or Rosslyn splits the difference.

It's midnight.

District Pizza Palace on S. Eads Street in National Landing runs to 2 a.m. Standards are lower at that hour and everyone knows it.

Fine print

What this is and isn't

This is desk research, not a tasting tour — it's assembled from local food press, the 50 Top Pizza rankings and restaurant listings, then organised into something you can act on. Nobody here ate anything.

Two things to check before you drive: addresses and hours move, especially for the multi-location groups, and at least one source used here dates from early 2024, so a listing being open then isn't proof it's open now. Ring ahead for anywhere out past the Beltway.

Notable omissions are deliberate. National chains with a NOVA branch — Jet's on East Glebe, Ledo, Vocelli — are real options and not interesting ones. And DC has its own tier, 2Amys and Della Barba among them, which is a different page.
