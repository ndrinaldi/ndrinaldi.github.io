Iceland in November

Five hours of usable daylight, weather that rewrites your plan twice a day, and the cheapest month to go that still has open roads. Here is what actually matters for a group of six.

Updated 16 Aug 4 min read

The short version

Go for eight nights, base in Reykjavík and one place on the south coast, and rent the 4x4 rather than the cheap car — the price gap is smaller than one cancelled day.

Light & weather

You get five hours, and you plan around them

Sunrise drifts from about 09:00 to past 10:30 across the month, and the light is gone by 17:00 at the start and 16:00 by the end. That is not a limitation so much as a schedule: everything outdoors happens in one block, and the rest of the day is driving, eating, and pools.

The aurora needs a clear sky more than it needs a dark one, and November clouds are the binding constraint. Treat any sighting as a bonus rather than a reason for the trip.

Getting around

The car decision is the whole trip

Everything else is reversible. This one is not, because the cheap category quietly removes the days you came for.

Recommended

4x4, studded

Handles the south-coast roads when they ice over, which they will at least once. Gravel and sand-and-ash cover included on most contracts.

The trap

Compact 2WD

Cheaper per day and fine in town. Loses you a day the first time a road closes and you have no legal alternative.

Shape of the week

Eight nights, two bases

Moving every night is how a group trip turns into a logistics job. Two bases, one long drive between them.

  • Nights 1–3 · Reykjavík Land, sleep off the redeye, do the city and the peninsula. Nothing ambitious on day one.
  • Night 4 · Drive east The long transfer. Four hours becomes six with stops, and in November it becomes six in the dark.
  • Nights 5–7 · South coast Waterfalls, the glacier lagoon, the black beach. This is the part people remember.
  • Night 8 · Back near the airport Do not try to drive from the south coast to a morning flight.

Questions

The things people always ask

Is it dark the whole time?

No. It is dark early. The usable block is roughly 10:00 to 16:00, and it is genuinely bright inside that window.

Do we need to book the Blue Lagoon in advance?

Yes, and further ahead than feels reasonable. The airport-adjacent slots on arrival and departure days go first.

Can we do this cheaper?

Two ways that actually work: cook most breakfasts and dinners, and drop from eight nights to six. Cutting the car category is the one saving that reliably costs more than it saves.
