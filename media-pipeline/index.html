How a show gets to the TV

Scroll

1

You want to watch something.

2

You add it to your watchlist.

how
Jellyseerr is the request app on your phone. You sign in with your Plex account, and it also watches your Plex watchlist, so a title added there becomes a request too. Each request is handed to Sonarr (TV) or Radarr (movies).
3

The librarians look it up in the index.

how
Sonarr and Radarr keep the wanted list and ask Prowlarr, the one index of every source, then pick the best copy using quality rules Recyclarr keeps current. Most copies come by Usenet (fast, paid, encrypted); what Usenet lacks comes by BitTorrent inside a VPN (Gluetun) that stops the download cold if the tunnel drops.
4

It downloads to the little box in the house.

how
The box is a Mac mini, always on, no screen. Every app here runs in its own Docker container inside a small Linux VM (Colima). SABnzbd or qBittorrent pulls the file onto the mini's small, fast disk; the librarian files it with a clean name and Bazarr fetches subtitles.
5

Then it moves up to the cloud.

how
The mini's disk is the hot tier: new files only. An hourly mover copies anything older than about six hours to Amazon S3, checks it, and removes the local copy. Both are merged into one library folder, so nothing else on the box notices the move.
6

You pick it on the TV. The TV asks the box.

how
Plex on the Fire TV or your phone asks Plex Media Server on the mini. Plex runs directly on the Mac, not in a container, so the chip can convert video for whatever device is asking. Tautulli sits beside it and logs what played and how; that is the assistant's window into the TV.
7

The box fetches it from the cloud and plays it on your TV.

how
Plex reads the file from S3 through CloudFront and streams it to the TV; the TV never knows which disk it came from. A Claude assistant lives on the mini too: it checks the whole chain every half hour, unsticks downloads, and takes Nick's asks from his phone through the Claude app. Nick and the assistant reach the box from anywhere through a private tunnel (Tailscale).
